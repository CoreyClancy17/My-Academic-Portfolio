# Teaching Experience

##  IDH1920H-18Fall 0220: Honors Symposium - The Evolution of Community
- Fall 2018
- Description: This course examines the concept of community from an interdisciplinary perspective with an emphasis on the ways that we experience, shape and create community.
- Course Objectives:
    1. Foster a sense of Honors identity and community within the Burnett Honors College
    2. Help students make a successful transition to university life and to Central Florida
    3. Provide students with an understanding of a variety of perspectives on community
    4. Require students to connect and evaluate themes from multiple perspectives through lectures and
    discussion
    5. Develop students’ ability to critically evaluate and communicate information
    6. Prepare students for active learning
    7. Educate students for lives of engaged citizenship


##  IDH1920H-19Fall 0242: Honors Symposium - Learning, Discovery, Engagement
- Fall 2019
- Description: This course highlights the Burnett Honors College themes of learning, discovery, and engagement. Creating the Honors and university communities are a priority. Building the foundations for academic and professional success are emphasized.
- Course Objectives:
    1. Foster a sense of identity and community within the Burnett Honors College and UCF
    2. Help students make a successful transition to university life
    3. Make campus connections through student groups and university resources
    4. Develop professional skills and values
    5. Develop students’ ability to critically evaluate and communicate information
    6. Expose students to undergraduate research, study abroad, and internship opportunities
